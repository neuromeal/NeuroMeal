ABOUT NEUROMEAL

Why NeuroMeal?

Making pharmacology and neuroscience easier to explore.

The idea

NeuroMeal is an independent educational project exploring the intersection of food, medication, biology, pharmacology, and neuroscience.

The project was created to explore a simple question: what actually happens inside the body when something we eat interacts with something we take as medicine?

Learn from simple to complex

Scientific information can become difficult to understand when explanations immediately jump into specialized terminology.

NeuroMeal therefore presents information in layers: starting with an accessible explanation, moving into biology, and finally exploring deeper pharmacological mechanisms for people who want to understand more.

Evidence matters

Medication information can have important consequences. NeuroMeal aims to use authoritative sources, official medication information, and scientific literature when developing its educational content.

Each profile and article will include sources so that readers can investigate the science themselves.

Educational disclaimer

NeuroMeal is an educational project and is not a substitute for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Information on this website should not be used to start, stop, or change a medication.

Medication information can change, and individual circumstances differ. Personal questions about medications and food interactions should be discussed with a qualified healthcare professional.
